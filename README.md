# Puffy Wings

**Puffy Wings** is a casual Android game developed and published by **Novera Labs**.

Take control of your wings, navigate through obstacles, and challenge yourself to achieve the highest score.

## Features

- Simple and engaging gameplay
- Easy-to-learn controls
- Endless challenge
- Score-based gameplay
- Designed for Android devices
- Available on Google Play

## How to Play

Tap the screen to control your character and keep flying.

Avoid obstacles, stay in the air, and try to achieve your highest possible score.

## Platform

- **Android**
- **Target SDK:** Android 16 (API 36)

## Google Play

Puffy Wings is officially published on Google Play by **Novera Labs**.

[Play Puffy Wings on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.novera.puffywings)
